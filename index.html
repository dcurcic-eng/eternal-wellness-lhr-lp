The treatment

How DiolazeXL works

DiolazeXL is an 810nm diode laser by InMode. Light energy passes into the skin and is absorbed by the melanin in the hair shaft, heating the follicle until it can no longer produce hair. Because hair grows in cycles, a full series runs about 8 treatments spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart.

70%
Average permanent hair reduction
What most patients see once the full series of about eight sessions is complete. Shedding starts one to two weeks after the first visit.
10 mm × 28 mm spot size, the largest in its class
Sapphire 3PC cooling: pre-cooling, parallel, post-cooling
Up to 3,000 watts of peak power
Selective photothermolysis targets melanin in the hair shaft
Calibrated for Fitzpatrick skin types I through VI

Questions, answered

Before you book.

Most areas need about 8 treatments spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart. Hair grows in cycles and the laser only disables follicles in their active growth phase, which is why the series matters more than any single visit.
DiolazeXL is significantly more comfortable than older laser systems. Its sapphire cooling chills the skin before, during and after each pulse; most people describe the sensation as a light rubber-band snap.
Yes. DiolazeXL is calibrated for Fitzpatrick skin types I through VI. We set the wavelength, fluence and pulse duration to your type at the consultation, and the sapphire cooling protects the surface of the skin while the energy reaches the follicle.
Small areas such as the underarms or upper lip run $100 to $250 per session, medium areas such as the bikini line $200 to $400, and large areas such as the legs, back or chest $400 to $800. Multi-session packages are discounted and financing is available through Cherry.
None. Most sessions take 15 to 45 minutes and you go straight back to your day. Mild redness or a warm feeling around the follicles is normal and usually settles within a few hours.
Treated hairs start to shed one to two weeks after the first session. Most patients notice a clear difference by the third or fourth visit, and the full result, around 70% permanent reduction on average, shows once the series is complete.
Yes, and you should. Shaving keeps the follicle intact, which is what the laser targets. Avoid waxing, plucking and epilating for the whole series, because those remove the root the laser needs.
It is permanent reduction rather than permanent removal. Most patients see about 70% fewer hairs after a full series, and what grows back is finer and lighter. An occasional maintenance session once or twice a year keeps the area clear.
